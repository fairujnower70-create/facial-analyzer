About Mien

Mien is a demo facial-analysis app. It detects a face in a photo or webcam frame, then estimates age, gender, expression, face shape, and left/right symmetry using on-device machine learning — nothing is sent to a server unless you choose to save a result.

How the analysis works

Detection and landmarking run in your browser using a compact neural network. Age, gender, and expression come from that same model family. Face shape and symmetry are computed from the geometry of 68 facial landmark points using simple ratio-based heuristics.

What Mien is not

Mien does not identify who someone is, and its outputs are statistical estimates, not facts. It isn't built or validated for medical, legal, hiring, law-enforcement, or any identity-verification use — please don't use it for those.

Your data

Photos are processed locally in your browser. If you sign up and tap "Save to history," only the computed results (and, if the image is small enough, a thumbnail) are stored against your account so you can review them later.

Estimates only — not a medical, biometric-identity, or legal tool.
