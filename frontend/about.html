About Mien

Mien is a facial-analysis web app. It finds faces in a photo, a group picture or a live camera frame, then estimates 11 expression scores, age and gender, face shape (Oval, Round, Square or Heart, each with a percentage) and left/right symmetry. The analysis runs on your own device, so nothing is sent to a server unless you choose to save a result.

What you can do

  • Scan one face or a whole group, with a separate result for every person.
  • Save a scan, or save every face in a group at once, each with its own thumbnail.
  • Follow your expression mix, symmetry trend and face shape mix on the dashboard, and keep a daily mood journal.
  • Keep scanning offline. Saved scans wait on your phone and upload automatically when you are back online.
  • Install Mien on your home screen and use the selfie-style mirrored camera.

How the analysis works

Face detection, 68-point landmarks, age, gender and seven expressions (happy, sad, angry, surprised, neutral, fearful, disgusted) come from compact pre-trained neural networks that are bundled with the app and run in your browser. Four extra scores (funny, excited, shy and contempt), the face shape percentages and the symmetry score are calculated by Mien from the geometry of the landmarks, using simple ratio-based rules. They are playful estimates, not model outputs, and they have not been validated on a labelled dataset.

What Mien is not

Mien does not identify who someone is, and its results are statistical estimates, not facts. Facial expressions do not reliably show what a person feels inside. Mien isn't built or validated for medical, legal, hiring, law-enforcement or identity-verification use, so please don't use it for those.

Your data

Photos are processed locally in your browser. If you sign up and tap "Save to history", only the computed results and a small thumbnail are stored against your account so you can review them later. You can delete any saved scan whenever you like. If you forget your password, you can request a reset PIN and an administrator can issue you a temporary password.

Estimates only — not a medical, biometric-identity, or legal tool.
