How it works

Four steps, no waiting room

From capture to a saved result in about a minute, on one screen.

1

Capture

Open your camera in a selfie-style mirror view, or upload a clear photo. Have a group? Drop in the whole photo.

  • Live camera
  • Photo upload
  • Group photo
2

Scan

The neon ring spins while 68 landmarks, expressions, age and face shape are worked out right on your device.

  • On-device AI
  • No upload
3

Review

See 11 expression scores, estimated age and gender, face shape percentages and a symmetry score, each face on its own card.

  • 11 expressions
  • Face shape %
  • Symmetry
4

Save & track

Save one scan or all faces at once. Watch your mood and symmetry change over time in your dashboard and journal.

Private by design

Your photo stays with you

The AI models run in your browser and are bundled with the app. Only the numbers (and a small thumbnail) are stored, and only if you tap Save.

Photo analysed on this device. Nothing uploaded by default.
